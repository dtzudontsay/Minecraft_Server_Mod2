## Conventions and research confidence

* **C (supported canon / published setting):** The place exists, historical status, known office or polity, named ruler, specific landmark, trade product or tradition. References point to *A Wiki of Ice and Fire*, which identifies its underlying novels, *The World of Ice & Fire*, and *The Lands of Ice and Fire*; verify direct source text before hard-coding contested chronology.
* **R (existing repo data):** Current `locations.json`, `locations_holdings.json`, `territory_zones.json`, `cultures.json`, `religions.json`, `landed_holdings.json`, `dynasties/essos_free_cities.json`. Repository entries are *pre-existing mod data*, not independent proof of canon.
* **M (mod planning estimate):** Populations, defenders, labor allocations, fertility, crops where not directly attested, terrain/building densities. No numeric range here is canon. Values are *scenario-generation envelopes*, not actual population counts.
* **U (unknown):** The historical source doesn't name the 298 AC incumbent, specific faith, household or exact quantity. Remain unresolved until researched or explicitly procedurally generated.
* **Ruin rule:** The **named city site** has *zero established permanent civic population and zero standing city garrison* unless a source establishes repopulation. Travelers, temporary camps, scavengers, robbers and separate adjacent settlements are **individual real NPCs** generated by distinct mechanics; they do not resurrect a city automatically.
* **Population scope:** Estimate is the named city/town and immediate built-up habitation only. Do not add a second surrounding province total on top without drawing explicit residence boundaries. Working shares apply to **employed/active adults**, not every resident. Naval crews and garrison members overlap the population: never double-count them.
* **Military scope:** Defender envelopes are suggested *resident individuals assigned to actual guard/army roles*. Historical army figures (such as the Three Thousand of Qohor) are not a verified 298 AC muster. Pentos' treaty restrictions are binding. Foreign mercenaries, ships and visiting khalasars must have separate actual members and an identified owner.
* **Religious/cultural demographics:** Dominant identifiers describe **priors for the generator**, not compulsory individual faiths. No unsupported canon percentages.
* **Place taxonomy:** `CITY`, `PORT_TOWN`, `FORTIFIED_TOWN`, `RUIN`, `SACRED_SITE`, etc., do **not** all produce vanilla villages. Iconic structures are protected landmarks.

## 2. Active settlement dossiers

### NWE-001 — Braavos (`braavos`)

- **C / people and governance:** Free City founded by escaped slaves; strong institutional opposition to slavery, maritime power, Iron Bank, guilds, keyholders; Sealord is elected/non-hereditary. Ferrego Antaryon is an explicitly named serving Sealord in the main-series period. **Do not assume his exact tenure at the *first hour* of 298 AC without chronology check**; reserve his character identity, house and office rather than creating a random competing Sealord. House Antaryon is in the repo's existing Braavosi dynasty set; other existing families include Prestayn, Reyaan, Fregar, Otherys, Zalyne and Nestoris. They are not all ruling royal houses. [S1][S2][S3]
- **M / people and employment:** 40–95k resident individuals; 1.5–5k guard/coastal defenders *drawn from residents*, plus separately inventoried warships/crews. Suggested employed-adult sector shares: fishing/food 12%, maritime transport/ship crews 18%, crafts/shipbuilding 24%, commerce/finance 22%, services/domestic 14%, government/religion/defense 10%. These shares are uncalibrated and must later split into individual worksite posts. Ruling families/keyholders are a **small social stratum**, not a separate counted occupation. Consider immigrant household and multi-faith composition.
- **C / economy and environment:** Fishing/shellfish, wharves, canals, sea trade, shipbuilding, Iron Bank credit, markets; laguna islands not credible large crop farms. Mainland/coastlands support limited supply; imports critical. Culture catalog prior `braavosi` with immigrant diversity; faith **mixed**, e.g. Moonsingers (`moonsingers`), Many-Faced God (`many_faced_god`), other temples including `rhllor`/`faith_of_the_seven` *as minorities*, not universal percentages. [S1]
- **C / construction & game connections:** Titan, Arsenal, many islands/canals/bridges, Purple Harbor, Ragman's Harbor, Chequy Port, Sealord's Palace, Iron Bank, House of Black and White, Isle of the Gods, Drowned Town. Special multi-district **harbor metropolis** rather than giant vanilla village. River/marsh and canal edges require engineered foundations. Politically independent; **do not give the city to House Antaryon as hereditary landed property** just because Ferrego is Sealord.
- **U:** Initial naval muster, precise citizen census, exact other religious proportions, founding/current magistrate composition and exact 298 first-day incumbent timeline.

### NWE-002 — Morosh (`morosh`)

- **C / government and economy:** Lorathi colony at Sarne delta on Shivering Sea. Fishing and **mining** are attested; linked commercial network with Saath. Lorathi authority, **unnamed** colonial governor (no new noble surname). [S4]
- **M:** 1.2–4.5k residents; 60–200 watch/guard. Employed adults: fishing and food 35%, mining/quarry 30%, port/transport 15%, crafts 10%, services 7%, guards/officials 3%; trading visitors additional real traveling NPCs. Local mixed `lorathi`/`sarnori` or other delta migrants possible, but no precise canon distribution. Faith **unknown/mixed**, *not* universally the extinct Boash cult.
- **M / farming:** Delta soils potentially productive where above flood/saltline, but waterlogging and salinity matter. River fish, coastal fish, imported grain, tentative barley/vegetable garden and livestock from surrounding drier ground. Floodplain terrain-validation mandatory.
- **Construction:** Working river mouth, wharves, warehouses, docks, fish-smoking buildings, mines inland, limited palisades, embankments and raised walkways. Site must snap to correct *delta bank*, not any nearest land.

### NWE-003 — Lorath (`lorath`)

- **C / governance and chronology:** Council of magisters actually rules; three elected princes (Harvest, Fisher, Streets) are now ceremonial. No named incumbents established. Historical Boash worship is **extinct** by this period; modern worship is mixed and cannot be treated as a universal cult of Boash. [S5][S6]
- **C / economy and society:** Fishing fleets, salted cod, whale oil, seal skins, walrus tusks; remote/poorer Free City with relatively weak war fleet, not a great land conquest power. [S5]
- **M:** 9–28k residents; 200–700 defenders and few military ships. Employed adults: fisheries/marine harvest 33%, processing/crafts 24%, shipping/trade 16%, services 17%, officials/religious 6%, security 4%. `lorathi` prior; mixed religions, individual choices. Arable stony isles low; very small garden patches, imported grain essential; mainland forestry outside town.
- **Construction:** Bleak stone islands and storm-exposed harbor; unusual **ancient mazemaker** stone mazes should survive as a separate archaeological layer. Port quarters, fish-curing yards, narrow lanes, ship shelter; avoid huge farmland footprints on the island.

### NWE-005 — Saath (`saath`)

- **C:** Surviving Sarnori city, at western Sarne delta; small **white-walled port**, residents identify as Tall Men / Tagaez Fen; continued survival helped by Ib and Lorath; commerce with Morosh; linked by Valyrian road. It is **not** an extension of Dothraki rule and not a ruined city. [S7]
- **U / government:** Present ruler and lineage unnamed. Represent a **local Sarnori polity/council** or unresolved office; don't fabricate a royal House Sarnor. Historical Sarnori kingship does not establish a named 298 AC king.
- **M:** 2.5–8k residents, 120–450 defenders. Employed-adult sectors: delta farming/pastoral/fishing 42%, crafts 19%, mercantile/river traffic 15%, services 12%, government/religion 5%, defense 7%. Culture `sarnori` exists in repo; religion specifically unknown, so no confident dominance assign; avoid automatically substituting Dothraki Great Stallion.
- **M / food:** Delta freshwater grains (barley/wheat where feasible), wetland livestock, river/coastal fish, possible flax/reeds for crafts, all *ecological proposals* not attested Saath crops. White walls, compact port, defensive approaches and scattered *independent* farm villages. Delta salinity zones need separate suitability tests.

### NWE-006 — Norvos (`norvos`)

- **C / authority:** **Bearded Priests** govern de facto through selection of magisters. Their deity's personal name is secret to initiates; use existing repo `bearded_priests` compatibility religion reference rather than invent an orthodox god. Named contemporary high priest/magisters **unknown**. [S8][S9]
- **C / geography and buildings:** Hills of Norvos; fortified upper city above lower riverside district, Sinner's Steps, three ritual bells Noom/Narrah/Nyel; limestone, pine/oak/beech woods; **terraced farms and small walled villages**. Both Upper Rhoyne and Noyne transport influence external holdings. [S8]
- **M:** 22–65k residents, 800–2.2k defensive troops/guards. Working adults: regional food production 24%, crafts 23%, river/caravan transport 18%, market/service 17%, religious/civic 12%, guard 6%. `norvoshi` culturally dominant, but travelers/mercenaries/marriages produce variations. `bearded_priests` institutional faith predominant in civic structure; other individuals can have other faiths. Crop candidates: wheat, barley, oats, pulses, vines on favorable slopes, livestock, logging; ecological estimates, not confirmed canon crop list.
- **Construction:** **Must** generate separate elevated fortified temple city and lower riverside city, connected by massive stone steps; independent farms and villages on surrounding terraces. Generic ground-level sprawling village would be incorrect.

### NWE-011 — Pentos (`pentos`)

- **C / government and named people:** Prince is chosen from powerful families but political power lies in wealthy magisters; a prince can be sacrificed after famine or lost war. **Name of current 298 AC prince not established**. Illyrio Mopatis is an important named **magister** and farm/orchard/mine owner in the surrounding Flatlands; he **must not be made prince**. Repo has `family_mopatis` with appropriately qualified lineage. [S10][S11]
- **C / treaty-critical military:** By treaty with Braavos, no regular standing army, no mercenary/free-company contracts, and **no more than twenty warships**. A large invented field army or 100-warship port would be incorrect. A limited local watch and guards can exist; keep their legal scope explicit. [S10]
- **C / culture/faith/economy:** Mixed Pentoshi (Andal, Valyrian and other heritage), mixed worship including R'hllor; wealthy traders, bazaars, harbor/shipping, wine, estates, orchards, farms and mines on the Flatlands, plus legally dubious indentured/bond servant arrangements despite nominal abolition. [S10][S11]
- **M:** 70–160k residents; 300–1.2k city/watch/estate protectors, **0 lawful standing field army**, maximum 20 warships (canon treaty ceiling; number actually present U). Employed adult sectors: regional field/garden 24%, craft/manufacturing 21%, shipping/trade 28%, household/services 18%, officials/temples 5%, permitted watch 4%. Elite magisters, nobles, servants and debt-bonded people are **social/legal statuses, not extra human counts**.
- **M / farming:** Nearby Flatlands favorable for orchards, vineyards, mixed grain, vegetables, horses, mines. Orchards and farms supported; individual crop details beyond wine are candidate ecology, not recorded canonical commodity counts.
- **Construction:** Massive high walls, square **brick towers**, tiled roofs, wealthy **walled manses**, Sunrise Gate, bazaars, port, large red temple and dense poorer districts. Estates/farms lie in the hinterland, not squeezed inside the city polygon.

### NWE-012 — Qohor (`qohor`)

- **C / institutions:** Walled city at edge of Forest of Qohor on Qhoyne. `black_goat` is the principal faith; R'hllor also has worshippers. Qohor is celebrated for elite forges and reworking Valyrian steel, tapestries, carvings, hunting, timber and long-range caravan trade. Its defense relies heavily on **Unsullied** soldiers after the *historical* Three Thousand of Qohor; that event does not establish today's exact garrison. [S12][S13]
- **U / politics:** No reliably named reigning 298 AC ruler from checked sources. Govern by city institutions/offices and merchants, rather than inventing a hereditary lord.
- **M:** 24–75k permanent residents, about 700–2.5k full defenders (many professional Unsullied), no claim that exactly 3,000 still serve. Sector shares: forestry/hunting/farming 23%, metalworking/woodcraft/tapestry 34%, trade/caravans/river freight 20%, domestic/service 12%, civic/temple 5%, guard 6%. `qohorik` majority; `black_goat` strong institutional/local faith but retain minority `rhllor` and immigrants. Resource suitability: forest timber, game, forest grazing, river fish, fields in cleared lowlands; ecological guesses for cereals, root crops, livestock.
- **Construction:** Strong walled riverside city, major forges, craft wards, markets, caravanserais, sacred Black Goat temples, river docks, timber yards; protected old-growth forest outside with a sparse network of work villages. **Valyrian steel reworking is a restricted skill held by specific identifiable NPC smiths**, not every smith or generic production bonus.

## 6. Step 2.2 integration and older-system rework backlog (NO code changes in this batch)

5. **Government and Houses:** connect historical rulers, magisters, councils, temples and families to actual office-holder people. 6. **Agriculture/economy:** crops, pasture and fisheries supported by suitable terrain and actual ownership/employer/inventory; wages, harvest labor and famine consequences belong to identifiable people/households. **Audit and retire** old anonymous farming and consumption authority when integrated.
7. **Military:** reserve actual person IDs as guards, Unsullied, crews and watch members. Qohor's historic Three Thousand is not a 298 headcount. Pentos standing-army ban and warship cap must be enforced. Threat estimates are an output of actual people/equipment/fortifications.
8. **Ruin-specific worldgen:** landmark/generation type cannot be inferred from population or printed-map-label size. A vast ruined Sarnath and a small functioning Saath require entirely different build plans.
9. **Cultural/religious sampling:** zone priors + household + upbringing + immigration + individual events; verify IDs from `cultures.json` and `religions.json`. Avoid overwriting existing named NPC identities.
10. **Tests / guardrails:** no duplicate sites across overlapping NWE–NE; no positive civic population at ruins; no identical NpcId assigned twice; no military manpower unrelated to actual individuals; no fictional prince superseding authored office; no religious/cultural assignment of extinct Boash faith to every modern Lorathi.

## 7. Unresolved research / authoring questions

- Validate exact marker centre and leader-line match against original `nwe.png` for all 16. `NWE-xxx` order is a working reference, not stable content identity.
- Map polygon ownership: require direct subzone geometry for Lorathi islands, Braavos coastlands, Sarne delta, Hills of Norvos, Qhoyne river and wooded lowlands. The repository currently has mostly broad zone envelopes.
- Culture IDs `lorathi`, `sarnori`, and the other seven-city references were verified in the current `cultures.json`. Step 2.2 should nevertheless validate the active runtime reference registry and fail on mismatches.
- Canon source chronology for Ferrego's accession vs the precise 298 AC campaign opening.
- Unnamed contemporary office holders of Lorath, Norvos, Saath, Morosh, Pentos and Qohor; generated NPCs should only be placed when office rules, lineage and current names are actually unresolved.
- Population demographic values need world-level calibration later: the above are *deliberately provisional ranges*, and regional and rural catchments must not be counted twice.
- Ferry/harbor footprints, terrain stabilization, climate, soil data and crop yield need terrain sampling when the worldgen planner is built. Numeric soil fertility and precise crop shares are not canonical.
